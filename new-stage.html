Advisor rules & tracking
1
At onboarding (Stage 1 kickoff), advisors assign each student their program track and share the 4-course minimum list. Students should select their 4 courses by Week 2 of GeekForce.
2
Progress is logged in the student's file. Completed courses and certifications are added to the resume and LinkedIn profile as part of Stage 1 profile optimization.
3
At each biweekly Stage 3 check-in, advisors review Upskill Track progress. Non-progress is flagged — a student not upskilling has less to show recruiters and their competitiveness drops week over week.
4
Certifications (Security+, AWS CLF, ISC2 CC, Google Advanced Analytics) are treated as priority completions — they unlock job applications that would otherwise be closed to the student.
5
4Geeks courses on 4geeks.com are the preferred starting point for budget-sensitive students. External free courses are the next default before paid external options.
