GeekForce Policy · Upskill Track Requirement
Requirement
All students must enroll in and complete a minimum of 4 courses from their designated Upskill Track within the duration of their job search period to participate in GeekForce. This requirement begins on graduation day and runs until job placement is confirmed.
Course selection
At the Stage 1 kickoff meeting, your advisor will share your program's course list. You must select your 4 courses and confirm them with your advisor no later than Week 2 of GeekForce.
Progress tracking
Upskill progress is reviewed at every biweekly advisor check-in. Completed courses and certifications must be reported to your advisor and will be added to your resume and LinkedIn profile as part of your career support.
Non-compliance
Students who are not making progress after two consecutive check-ins will receive a formal re-engagement notice. Continued non-compliance may result in suspension or removal from GeekForce, including loss of Job Guarantee eligibility where applicable.
Why this matters: The tech job market moves fast. This requirement exists to keep you competitive and hireable throughout your job search — not as a burden, but as a direct investment in your placement outcome.

Internal proposal — 4Geeks curriculum team
AI Engineering grads need intermediate-to-advanced courses that don't exist on 4geeks.com yet.
The current 4geeks.com catalog covers beginner AI (Prompt Engineering, AI Fluency, No-Code & Automations) but AI Engineering bootcamp graduates are already past that level — they've shipped agents, set up MCP servers, and built FastAPI backends. This is a product gap and a revenue opportunity. Students in the job search period are highly motivated and would pay for advanced content that directly maps to what employers are hiring for.
Suggested courses to develop
RAG & Vector Databases
Grads built basic agent loops but never touched embeddings, vector stores (Pinecone, Chroma), or retrieval pipelines. This is the #1 skill gap in AI engineering candidates right now.
Production Agents with LangChain/LangGraph
Grads know OpenClaw — employers hire for LangChain/LangGraph. This course bridges their hands-on agent experience to the industry-standard framework they'll find on every job description.
LLM Fine-Tuning & Evaluation
LoRA, PEFT, model evaluation metrics, RLHF basics. Not covered in the bootcamp. Differentiates engineers who consume models from those who shape them.
MLOps & LLM Deployment at Scale
Grads can deploy a FastAPI — they need to go further: model serving, monitoring, cost optimization, CI/CD for AI systems. Critical for mid-level AI engineering roles.
Building MCP Servers (not just connecting)
Grads configured MCP connections — the next level is authoring custom MCP servers. Anthropic's standard is becoming the default for AI tool integration across the industry.
AI Safety & Responsible Deployment
Bias, hallucination mitigation, red-teaming, governance frameworks. Increasingly required in enterprise AI job specs and a genuine differentiator in interviews.

Advisor rules & tracking
1
At onboarding (Stage 1 kickoff), advisors assign each student their program track and share the 4-course minimum list. Students should select their 4 courses by Week 2 of GeekForce.
2
Progress is logged in the student's file. Completed courses and certifications are added to the resume and LinkedIn profile as part of Stage 1 profile optimization.
3
At each biweekly Stage 3 check-in, advisors review Upskill Track progress. Non-progress is flagged — a student not upskilling has less to show recruiters and their competitiveness drops week over week.
4
Certifications (Security+, AWS CLF, ISC2 CC, Google Advanced Analytics) are treated as priority completions — they unlock job applications that would otherwise be closed to the student.
5
4Geeks courses on 4geeks.com are the preferred starting point for budget-sensitive students. External free courses are the next default before paid external options.
